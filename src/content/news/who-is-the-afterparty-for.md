## Closing time

Every night out has a moment when the lights come up and the question changes from "what's next?" to "where now?". For a lot of London, the honest answer is nowhere.

## Where people go

Without late venues, the night disperses into flats, kitchens and night buses. Some of that is lovely. Some of it is just a city with fewer shared places to be.

## What would help

Later licences for small venues, better night transport and a little more trust in the people who run grassroots rooms well.
