## Intro

Most techno records are written for the peak. Pressure Systems is written for what comes after it: the cab queue, the first bus, the walk home with your ears still ringing.

## The sound

The palette is narrow on purpose. Dub delays, a single detuned synth and field recordings from what sounds like a railway arch at closing time. It should get monotonous. It doesn't, because Rook keeps moving things by inches.

> A record that trusts you to lean in.

## Key tracks

Opener "Low Front" sets the tone with seven minutes of slowly gathering weather. "Isobar (Night Version)" is the closest thing to a club track. "Still Air" closes things with almost no rhythm at all.

## Verdict

Not a record for everyone, and not trying to be. For anyone who has stayed for the last record and the lights coming up, it might be the most honest dance album of the year.
