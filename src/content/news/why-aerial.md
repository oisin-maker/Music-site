## The small rooms

Most of the music London is proud of started somewhere with low ceilings and a bar at the back: a pub function room, a basement, a railway arch. Those rooms are where bands play their first gigs and DJs learn to read a crowd.

## What's happening to them

Pubs and clubs across the city have closed or come under pressure in recent years. Each one that goes takes a stage with it, and most grassroots gig venues are pubs.

## What we'll do

Aerial will review records, gigs and DJ sets; pick the best nights in small venues each week and put them on a map; and pull in the news from the rest of the scene so you don't have to go looking.

## Get involved

If you run a venue, promote a night or write about music, get in touch. And if you just love going out in London, sign up to the newsletter at the bottom of the page.
